Info

I shoot film and stills for brands, mostly things that move fast.

Born and raised in the Alps, in the province of Sondrio, and now based in Milan. I studied Public Relations and Business Communication at IULM.

I work freelance with production companies on projects for brands including Lamborghini, Ducati, Moncler and Kappa. On a shoot I take on whatever the job needs: directing, camera, edit, colour, or all of it on the smaller ones.

Between commissions I shoot LifeTalks, and I ride, ski and kite as much as the calendar allows, which is usually where the personal work comes from.
