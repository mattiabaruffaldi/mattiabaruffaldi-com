Info

I shoot film and stills for brands, mostly things that move fast.

Born and raised in the Alps, in the province of Sondrio, and now based in Milan. I graduated in Public Relations and Business Communication at IULM.

I started out as a photography assistant to Alberto Alquati, and came to moving images through Fabrizio Accettulli of RGB Films.

I have worked with Unreal Media House since the beginning, and it is still where most of my work comes through: projects for Lamborghini, Ducati, Moncler and Kappa, among others.

Between commissions I shoot LifeTalks, and I ride, ski and kite as much as the calendar allows, which is usually where the personal work comes from.
