LifeTalks

A project of my own, outside of client work: telling the lives of ordinary people who turn out to have an uncommon story. Shot when I have the time, which is why there are two.

Episode 1

Grazia Robustellini

My grandmother. Grazia is a sprightly 88-year-old from Tirano, in the Valtellina valley. I hope her stories land with you the way they landed with me.

Episode 2

Michelangelo “Puki” Pochettino

Puki is one of the significant figures of the vintage motorcycle world in Italy. Former motocross racer, distributor for Montesa and importer of CZ, he keeps one of the most varied collections in the country.
